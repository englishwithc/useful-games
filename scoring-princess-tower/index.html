    Royal rules

    How to Play

    1. Guess the next card. Is it higher or lower than the card you can see?
    2. Know the order. Ace = 1 (lowest), Jack = 11, Queen = 12, King = 13 (highest). A tie is safe.
    3. Build the tower. Every correct guess rolls the green die and adds 1–6 stones.
    4. Mind your hearts. A wrong guess rolls the red die: it always costs exactly 1 heart, plus up to 3 stones.
    5. Steady the wobble. Every 3 correct guesses a shape puzzle appears. Press Start, watch the glowing stones, then tap them in the same order. Success earns a bonus green-die roll; failure costs a heart.
    6. Reach the top. You begin with 5 hearts. Lose them all and the tower falls — your height goes on the local Top 5 leaderboard.

    Tip: press H / ↑ for Higher and L / ↓ for Lower. Esc pauses.
