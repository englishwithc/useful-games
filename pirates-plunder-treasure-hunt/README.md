# 🏴‍☠️ Pirate's Plunder Treasure Hunt 🏴‍☠️

## Overview

**Pirate's Plunder** is an interactive classroom tool designed to motivate students by turning task completion into an exciting treasure hunt game. Teachers can use this during lessons to reward students, keeping them engaged and motivated to complete assignments.

---

## 🎯 How It Works

### The Classic Mode
In the standard game mode, students flip through cards looking for hidden treasures. Each card reveals either:
- 💰 **The Treasure** - Win! Students found what they were looking for!
- 🏴‍☠️ **A Pirate** - A challenge that adds difficulty to the hunt
- 💨 **Empty Space** - Just another card to flip

### The Cursed Mode
In the cursed mode, students face increased difficulty with 3 pirates instead of just 1. This creates a more challenging experience when students need extra motivation!

---

## 👨‍🏫 Teacher Implementation Guide

### Reward System

This game is designed as a **task completion reward system**:

| Task Completed | What Happens |
|---------------|--------------|
| Student completes 1 task | Flip 1 card |
| Student completes 2 tasks | Flip 2 cards (in some modes) |
| Student completes all tasks | Complete the treasure hunt! |

### How to Use in Class

1. **Start Each Lesson**: Click "Restart Game" to begin a fresh adventure
2. **Track Progress**: As students complete tasks, they earn card flips
3. **Encourage Participation**: Students flip cards themselves during lessons
4. **Celebrate Wins**: When treasure is found, celebrate the achievement!
5. **Adjust Difficulty**: Switch between Classic and Cursed mode based on student needs

### Classroom Benefits

- ✅ **Gamifies Learning** - Turns mundane tasks into an exciting adventure
- ✅ **Visual Progress Tracking** - Students can see their progress immediately
- ✅ **Flexible Timing** - Works for any duration of lesson
- ✅ **Engaging Atmosphere** - Keeps students focused and motivated
- ✅ **Adaptable Difficulty** - Classic mode for beginners, Cursed mode for advanced learners

## 🎮 Game Modes Explained

### Classic Mode (Default)
- **1 Pirate** on the board
- Standard difficulty
- Perfect for: Daily tasks, homework completion, participation
- Best for: Most students in most situations

### Cursed Mode ⚠️
- **3 Pirates** on the board
- Increased challenge
- Perfect for: Extra credit, bonus tasks, or when you need extra motivation
- Best for: Students who need a bigger challenge or when class is quiet

---

## 🚀 How to Play

1. Click on any card to flip it over
2. Look for the treasure 💰 hidden among the cards
3. Avoid the pirates 🏴‍☠️ if you want to win!
4. If you find the treasure, celebrate your victory!
5. If a pirate appears the treasure moves to a new location

---

## 🔧 For Teachers

- Start with Classic Mode to build confidence
- Use Cursed Mode when students need extra motivation
- Keep the game visible on your screen during lessons
- Celebrate treasure finds loudly and enthusiastically!
